## Project Sign-up
From 1st to 3rd October: project bidding opens at 9am for the team admin ONLY (me).

An expression of interest consists of CVs, written expression of interest, and a 10-minute pitch video (more playful, worth putting work in).
Pitch day: teams call online: answer 10-20mins of questions on MS Teams

The project allocation is based on sponsor/team preferences, the average team rank across all pitches, the priority, and an optimisation process.
