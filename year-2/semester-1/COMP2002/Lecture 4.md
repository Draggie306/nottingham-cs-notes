## Group Report and Manuals 

![](../../../Images/Pasted%20image%2020260126161207.png)

Strongly recommended to build the user manual/“how-to-use” within the software itself to help people understand what has been done. It doesn’t have to be big at all, or overly formal: keep is short, clear and concise (e.g. examples on standard things). If it gets too long, the software design might not be very good.

Manual is designed to describe the main design and implementation interfaces.

It is recommended for provenance reasons to download the template from Moodle.


Test documentation: there shouldn’t be a paragraph for every test, but instead some statistics about coverage, dependent on a tool, etc.
Manual testing: if the product does not support automatic testing, more writing may be required to explain what a test suite is doing.


### Showcase: Wednesday 13th May

![](../../../Images/Pasted%20image%2020260126161953.png)

The showcase will be described in more detail towards the Easter holidays. 

Face to face: a video presentation, then a face to face discussion/live demonstration, followed by a Q&A over 20 minutes. There will be 1 or 2 other teams, staff members to assess, and some more for “fun”. 

> *”I was at Oxford before, they think they’re really good, but the software presented here is even better.”*

It is attended by your own supervisors, 40+ external visitors and 50+ academics, and students.

It is evaluated with a first marker, 

There are 2 people marking each session stream, 1 person marking each stall, a panel with second marking, and public voting.

## Deliverables 

### Individual report
- Your view of the project: what you do did, why you were useful, explaining yourself versus anything frustrating, how the project changed from the description of it.

- Your role: what role you took on (not just formal role like Team Admin, but backend/UI/etc.), contributions made (it helps to have done something, e.g. backend/UI), what difficulties there were (think about them sensibly: if half the team were away, there are no marks knocked out if there is a challenging situation so think about how we coped with the challenge)

- Reflection on working in a group. Things you do that are annoying that you wouldn’t do again, things that others do. How it went and how difficulties that crop up are resolved. Talk about attendance. 


### Peer review

- Be specific: “worked hard on the UI and implemented all the 3D code”. Being specific and concrete is useful

The submitted peer review is marked and will be used to scale marks. Good peer reviews scale the group score accordingly
