

Regular recurring meetings: 
- Every member is expected to be present during the meetings
- Every team member should contribute equally.

Expectations:
- Initial prototype to demonstrate that the plan works (or learn otherwise)

Ownership over the project is required: it is not the business sponsor or team leader to lead: they are the customers. 

Expected to use SCRUM processes and Git - the repositories provided, which will be used as evidence. 

![](Pasted%20image%2020251027111549.png)

Product backlog:
- user story: AS a [role], I want [feature] so that [result].
	- Detailed, acceptance criteria, used as a story point
- Tasks: items that provide indirect value
- Bugs: continuous bug fixing throughout

Sprint backlog:
- Some items from the product backlog to address in the next increment.

### Sprint Planning

Team decides what goes in, and the result is a lot of story points

Stand-ups (scrums) should be no lesss than twice weekly. Every member should say what they have done since the last scrum, what they are going to commit to get done, and if any problems are stopping progress. 

They should be locked to 15 minutes to not take too much time.
