### When to start applying 
![Pasted image 20251007132138](../Images/Pasted%20image%2020251007132138.png)

Large companies: already advertising. Medium and smaller companies will be offering later on.

Most people: apply to 30-50 places and will receive one offer. Keep applying even during interviews and second stage interviews. Apply until you feel tired.


### Interview types
1. Video interview with camera
2. HR interview - commitment to the job, commitment to the organisation.
3. Some of the time: coding assessment - a few leetcode questions

Employers are more interested in your capability and being interested in learning - could you do this for 8 hours a day. 


- Here’s what I’ve done
- Here’s what I’ve done in a team
- Here’s how I manage my time

Prove quality over quantity: showcase having time for enthusiasm and motivation to apply for the job, avoid “one-click applications” (often no person on the end of it; they do not show the detail you need to persuade the employer).

Researching about the employer is underrated: do some research on “about us” pages, social media posts, on the news recently, projects they have been working on. Relating this to you: say “been interested in this company, saw on youtube/career show”

> **Always save applications to refer to during the recruitment process: spreadsheets, colour coding, etc.**

Pick and choose elements of a CV for some roles where appropriate (e.g. more Python for Python-based programs)
