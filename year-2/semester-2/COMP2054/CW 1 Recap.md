
## Algorithm analysis
### Counting primitive operations
It is not important to give an exact number for complex assignments, such as `c = A[i]` - this could be 5 operations or just 2. The exact number is unimportant.

### Runtime estimations

Calculating a function that initialises a value from another (2 operations), runs a for loop `n` times (with 7 operations inside), and returns a value (1 operation), will have a total number of operations as: **7n + 3**.
