
Taught by: Andrew Parkes working in optimisation - Room C78

- Algorithms: a step-by-step procedure for solving a problem in a finite amount of time. No magic steps allowed.
- Data structures: lists, binary trees, heaps, hashmaps, graphs.
- Efficiency: methods to analyse, classify and describe algorithms’ efficiency, to select the “best algorithms”.


25% coursework: 3 in-lab tests. Generally, everything included up until the week before the test. Most questions test the material directly but about 20% is stretch to show deeper understanding. Top 2 of 3 are used to calculate grade.

### Study Skills
- Often problems arise from going too fast.
- Backtracking is normal. Often, re-read definitions from the start, and restart the entire question.
- ”Fix a bug in your thinking” - makes things more efficient even if backtracking

- Do all exercises in slides - they are likely to be part of an exam question
- Focus on the basics first


### Aims
To understand key issues in program design, with good knowledge of some common algorithms and data structures.

Designing data structures and algorithms that express this functionality to make efficient use of **time**, **memory**, **CPU usage**, and to evaluate a given implementation in terms of its efficiency.
