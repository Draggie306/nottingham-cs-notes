
Fuzzy Sets focus on ambiguity and precision, with the question: "*Humans do not have the best vision, hearing, smell and thinking, but can reason well under pressure. How can we emulate this?*" Lotfi Zadeh publishes ‘Fuzzy Sets’ in Information and Control, vol.8, pp. 338-353

Fuzzy sets are designed to capture vague and uncertain information, mimicking the type of uncertainty which people use daily (“its quite warm”).

Mamdani inference was the first form of fuzzy inferencing. It turns a numeric input into a fuzzy set, using singleton fuzzification: the signleton set has membership of 1 where that number is, and 0 everywhere else.

Defuzzification: can turn into another number or a linguistic symbol.
