Heuristic: a set of criteria or rules. 

Advantages: cheaper, no users needed, identifies major problems, 

Disadvantages: relies on interpretation of heuristics, can't cover every type of interface and requires nuance from users' opinions


At every stage of action:
- Is the goal clear
- Is it clear the appropriate action obvious (is the slider intuitive)
- Is it clear the appropriate action leads to the goal? 
- What problems may the user face in performing the action? (exit strategy, back button, etc.)
