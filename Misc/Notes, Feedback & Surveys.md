### SEM Survey: DMS 
7) The workload associated with this module is manageable.
7a) You selected 'disagree' or 'strongly disagree' with this statement. Please tell us why.

Whilst the workload for this module on its own is manageable, the way it is timetabled in with others quickly makes this very difficult to achieve. Whilst there was some help given in terms of using a Gantt charts to organise when is best to achieve all deadlines, this is fairly unrealistic combined with the formal reasoning and operating systems and concurrency workload, which took priority due to shorter, more immediate deadlines. What’s more is that the Gantt chart overran inti the Christmas vacation period anyway which was a little odd to see given the statements that lecturers “didn’t want students working over the holidays”.  all of this combined letter only having six days off during the Christmas vacation period with many days working for 7-10 hours straight with purely coding. This, combined with many full-time days over several weeks spent in the lab prioritising the other modules to try and get ahead before Christmas, unfortunately led to mild burnout, stress and knock-on effects for family.

8) Please use the space below for any constructive comments about this module

 I found a large majority of the lecture content to be fairly superfluous and largely unnecessary. It felt like much of the time, most of the content was filler. Additionally, when it came to the more difficult topics such as Java FX migration, there was very, very little information given in slides, resulting in many additional hours of work being spent on researching the refactor. Whilst this is expected to a degree, when the overall time to produce excellent work up to my standards required over 112 hours of work,, on this module which should have only taken 100 hours for coursework, it would’ve been much appreciated if more content could have been made instead of just pointing to a premade YouTube videos by someone else. Lots of work came very down to the wire and there seems to be no leniency applied for rendering, uploading and submitting video links for the final submission. The early deadline bonus was never stated to be 5% of mark achieved or 5% if the entire grade by the time this deadline was reached, which made organising difficult - it might have been worse going for if it was a 5% of little of the entire entire grade, but a 5% uplift of the mark that you achieved I might not be worth it. The tasks were also extreme disproportional and could be inhabited in many different ways, which I hope is accounted for during the marking process. For example, Task 4 took the same amount of time as all the other tasks 1-8 combined, at over 55 coding hours; I spent additional effort in refactoring it in a modern way, which I fear will not be recognised by the marker, who may just be assuming that we use the old inefficient canvas paint method. Many students also use AI for automating the refactor (overheard several times) creating a broken looking game but with credible-looking code, which I fear will disproportionately affect how my own code and codebase I tactically and evidently designed over several weeks is perceived and marked.
