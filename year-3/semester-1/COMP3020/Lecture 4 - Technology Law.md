


## Laws

1) Compel: it can fine employers, imprison people
2) Are specific
3) Are territorial: depends on where people are, not the servers.
4) Lag: laws are written after the harm happens.

### Where laws originate

- Parliament: create statutes; acts and regulations.
- Courts: case law. What statutes actually mean when applied in a case.
- Regulators: codes and guidance, and are what generally judge users in practice.
- Elsewhere: EU AI act, GDPR.

### Data protection

- Is it personal data?
- What is the lawful basis?
- Have people been told in terms they understand?
- How long is the data being kept for and why so?
