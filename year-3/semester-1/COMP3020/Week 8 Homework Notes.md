

## Case study: Telegram

Telegram has over a billion monthly users. It is a messaging/broadcasting app, with an emphasis on security and privacy. Its ToS states it will remove illegal public content, but not politically motivated censorship. 

This stance on freedom of speech was publicly demonstrated in 2018 during a famous rebuttal against the Russian government for user data. This has made it very popular for activists, protest groups, undercover operations and press organisations. 

However, it is not end-to-end encrypted by default, unlike other apps such as Signal. Features like "secret chats" are, however.

Because of is privacy-first design, it has also become useful for criminal activities. 


## Definitions

An ethical "friction" is a **tension between two or more moral obligations, legitimate pressures or values**, which is usually **resolvable through better processes**, communication, negotiation, or time, but which can cause real discomfort along the way.  

An ethical "dilemma" is a situation where **two or more external, organisational or practical obligations conflict** in a way that means satisfying one necessarily means failing another.
