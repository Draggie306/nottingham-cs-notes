## Project Proposal

It is typically structured with 4 pages of A4 paper:

1. Project Title
2. Background and Motivation
3. Aims and Objectivies
4. Work Plan
	- Describes the tasks to be carried out, time scales, deliverables and key dates (interim, final report etc)
	- The time plan should be realistic, not idealistic. It should take into account other commitments including exam periods and holidays
	- The work plan allows you to see what is involved: feasability, to monitor progress and see if you are behind schedule.
	- For the submission deadline for the interim report, there is still a chance to change it, but the argument about *why* the change was required is needed.
5. Bibliography


The aim is usually a single sentence ...

For proposals, 3/4 references are fine

The time estimation for coding is very difficult. A small prototype that is extrapolated is very useful




### Components of a good proposal

- Good technical writing style
- Appropriteness of the aim and objectives
- Indication of knowledge on the subject area and topic
- Indication of critical thinking, e.g. comparing multiple papers.

- Quality of the work plan - extent to which the structure is broken down, viability of the sequence, realistic timetables, complete coverage of expected deliverables, justifications of the work and time plan.
- Assess all threats to the completion of the project work, minimising risks and taking precautions and alternative routes to recovery. Can user testing be conducted easily and at all times?
