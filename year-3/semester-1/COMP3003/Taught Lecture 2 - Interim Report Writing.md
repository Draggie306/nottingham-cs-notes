## Recap

Projects are student-led. The dissertation marking criteria highlights this: supervisor should not be leading meetings, just giving ideas. Supervisors come in if you get stuck.

Meetings should be roughly an hour during the first weeks, then reduces as they are student-led: email can be given instead to report progress.

Always prepare for meetings, not a chat. Decide what is needed: advice on something or feedback on something to initiate a discussion. Good idea to give things to supervisor in advance so they have time to read/comment on it. 

This is a 40-credit module: 1/3 of your time, 12 hours per week. This depends on coursework load and working pattern with a **substantial amount of work**. 


## Proposal

> Elements in each component are mostly reusable. This is not plagarism, as they are different items.

An interim report consists of an outline with progress updates and draft content - it is somewhat an extended version of the proposal.

Interim reports are marked by the supervisor, and is worth 10% of the overall grade. The deadline is exactly 3pm on December 8.

The word limit is around 5,000 words over 15 pages of A4 paper (assumption: 500 words/page, with more space allowed for e.g. title page, illustrations and bibliography). Assessment criteria includes whether the report is at the limit and there may be some margin of error, but there is a reason why these are limits.
