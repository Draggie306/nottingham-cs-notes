## Part 2: Introduction to Conversational UI/UX

CUI ("cooey") is a conversational user interface. Can refer to text-based chatbots, or voice-based VUIs (Voice User Interface), or hybrid versions of GUIs + Voice, such as Siri.

UX = user experience. It is a broader term encompassing experience, that can evolve over time, that involves how the ecosystem of information is structured: it is not just design of interfaces and information. UX design is a profession encompassing web design, app design, game design, graphics design and more. HCI research is largely an academic discipline, but there is an overlap.

There are many conversational interfaces: robotics, conversational robots, domestic robots - but the uptake is very low. 

With hands-free environments, such as cars, it makes make more sense to have a voice interface than with a graphical touchscreen.

#### How VUIs technically work
User: "Alexa remind me to fix my clock"

ASR: transcribes audio via the microphone. This happens until it is triggered by the "wakeword", such as "Alexa". It does not record anything until this is triggered. Once active, it begins recording until there is a pause. 



Challenges: voice is very personal, and can reflect where users are from, dialect, sex/gender, and issues around privacy and surveillance (intimate settings).
