
## Part 1

The danger of bots in the age of AI:

Bots can be "superspreaders". Some studies of tweets identify 6% of accounts were identified as bots and spread 31% of "low-credibility" information. It takes only 2-10 seconds from the appearance of fake news 

Targeting influences is a common technique: targeted influences who supported Catalonia's independence referendum with negative and violent content, known as **social hacking**.


## Part 2: Filter Bubbles

**Online, the most valuable currency is attention.** Self-reinforcing echo chambers that only show content based on your views, likes, friends. Other views are sieved out and people are left in their own echo chamber. Things like hoaxes, conspiracy theories and news gets propagated and go viral, then break out once it is too large to contain.

Recommendations are a way to lure viewers based on personalised data (likes, history, location). There is a psychological reward for participation: reshares, likes and comments result in dopamine-like responses like addictive substances.
