## Marking

Y2 and Y3 - differentiate between Y1 with more advanced topics that assess CS, not just formative assessments. Still building on a strong foundation.

30% get firsts.

Overall, students who obtain 50% in a few modules can still get first-class marks if other modules average out to a good mark.

Marking has at least 16 different steps.

Moderation is also done, involving markers’ distributions; if there has been a large change in marks after marking, more rigorous documentation and reviewing occurs.

Marks are rarely and, if so, carefully, changed. It is often that, if there is a sudden 20% change, then there is an issue with the mark scheme or a specific marker/group. For the latter, readjustments will be in line with everyone to be fair.

Most queries about disagreements are to do with academic judgement rather than marking errors. Academic judgement is not grounds to review/appeal a mark.

To prevent issues, there is a new query form which will require a response to become more transparent. Everyone is allowed to use a form including asking for more feedback/mark breakdown/clarity.

If a mark on Computer Security COMP3006 is reduced by 10% (e.g. from 70% to 60%), their overall degree change will be -0.2%, which will almost certainly be rounded back.


## Group Projects

This is about software engineering - not just programming. There are good teammates who can and cannot program well. 

In Semester 1:
- Project signup - list of projects on Moodle to sign up for
- Competitive bidding:
	- Making our team sound attractive to those offering the project.
	- Individual deliverable: CV submission
- Project allocation; often not the one at the top of the team’s list.
- Development 1, resulting in a:
	- proof of concept - to show to the employer/academic
	- initial report
	- Individual deliverable: interim peer assessment

In Semester 2:
- Development 2 - creating the finished product
- Final product, resulting in:
	- Showcase
	- Repository published
	- Group reports (what the group has done + documentation)
- Individual deliverable: Final peer assessment (how teammates did) and individual report
